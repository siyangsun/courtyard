---
title: Arrangement learnings from remaking Jonas Brothers' SOS
---
- phrases/sections dont have to be mod4 bars, both on a micro and macro level
- this goes for different instrumental elements as well
- switching up the drum sound or adding an extra layer on a melody can do a ton for how something feels
- small changes on verse2 vs verse1 or chorus2 vs chorus1 are necessary to feel progression
- (already knew this) backing vocals can add a lot of interesting palette to any other element, doesnt have to just layer on the main vocal
- vocal delivery changes also add a sense of progression
