---
title: Camelot Wheel
---
The camelot wheel is a system developed to make harmonic mixing easier for beginners, translating keys into numerical codes that traverse the [[circle of fifths]] by going up or down a number, and switch to a [[relative minor]] by going from A to B. This lets you easily identify songs in a similar key to transition between without having to remember the music theory behind it.
