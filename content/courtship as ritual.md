---
title: Courtship as Ritual
---


rituals of courtship are deeply entwined with the gender roles of a given society
## arrangement
- arranged by family
- matchmaking

## origins of romantic love
- originally thought to be eurocentric in origin
- celebrated in ancient china
- happens in various isolated tribal cultures: https://www.jstor.org/stable/3773618

## rituals

#### tokens of love
- finnish puukko knives
- welsh lovespoons
- "Chivalrous gentlemen in England often sent a pair of gloves to their true loves. If the woman wore the gloves to church on Sunday it signaled her acceptance of the proposal."
- rings
- locks of hair in victorian England
- letters
- money

#### courtship events
- debutante ball
- cotillion
- someone else's wedding
	- catching the bouquet
	- breaking a loaf on the bride's head and scrambling for crumbs
- parties with a social circle

#### social games
- missing the last train
- hanging out / "talking stage"
	- bifurcates into either "asking out" or "sliding in" pathways to exclusivity
	- https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11133-019-09430-2

#### formal confession
- asking someone out
- kokuhaku
- "DTR"
- "hard launch"
- "facebook official"

#### rejection
- can occur based on quality (or lack of quality) of the ritual - e.g. a shitty gift or a cringe confession
- "friendzoning"
- gender-specific safety concerns
- in china, passionate love is viewed in a darker, more negative light
	- many of the phrases associated involve sadness
- PSL (parasocial love) and UL (unrequited love)
- divorce as a "certificate of happiness" https://www.eastasiaforum.org/2023/06/16/divorce-as-a-certificate-of-happiness-in-modern-china/
- status shifts caused by rejection

#### marriage
- rings, vows
- "jumping the broom" in black communities and cajun communities
	- bringing the families together

## societal barriers

#### roles in the community
- modern china - role as a member of the state first. focus on how they'll fit in and be viewed by the community, over how they themselves feel
- status requirements / social standing
#### eloping against authority
- romeo and juliet, obviously
- also spoken about in tribal cultures around the world

## unique words for love
- in igbo, the rough translation is "I see you with my eyes"
