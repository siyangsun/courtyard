title: Difficulties of building in public
---

- WIP is less polished and not as "good"
- too frequent of updates can feel spammy
- hard to remember to compile updates while working; also can break focus if it's not within workflow to do so (e.g. writing notes for non-text formats)
- inconvenient to retroactively document processes for projects already in motion
