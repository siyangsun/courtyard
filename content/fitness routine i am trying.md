minimum effort routines and exercises

diet to aim for: 
- 2300 calories a day
- 100-120g protein
- good amount of fiber
- avoiding eating dinner too late

1x a week: swim
2x a week: try to lift weights for a little bit

minimum daily calisthenics:
- 3x10 pushups, squats, or lunges
- 3x10 of one of these core exercises:
	- dead bug
	- russian twist
	- bird dog
	- crunches with rotation
expand it if i have capacity or am skipping one of the longer workouts
