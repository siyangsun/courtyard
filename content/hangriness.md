prandial - related to eating meal (postprandial=post-meal)
nadir - lowest point
syncope - passing out
whipple's triad - used to determine insulinoma. hypoglycaemia, low blood plasma glucose concentration, and relief of symptoms when plasma glucose concentration is increased
secretagogue - substance that causes another substance to be secreted
