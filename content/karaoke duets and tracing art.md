---
title: Karaoke duets and tracing art
---

i have a friend who likes to trace art but never learned drawing. because it's less about trying to understand where the original artist came from, and more about just following along and having fun with them in a low stakes way. it took me a long time to understand this idea and to understand this friend.

some friendships are like this too
two people just following along with each other's lives and having fun together, but without truly understanding each other or walking in each other's shoes
you just spend time together, laugh and sing duets and hold hands

you trace the outlines that they are without tracing the steps that took them there

(there's nothing wrong with friends like this - after all, some of the purest friendships we have are of this nature)

but sometimes, with the internet - the traces of your friend's past selves will come looking for you

old acapella tryouts, poetry under an alias offhandedly mentioned, the "close friends" instagram story you were inexplicably added to, a name on a college activist group's blog



maybe all of this IS me trying to understand this friend
looking at your traced artwork, with the scratchy unconfident lines (like watching a kid pedal madly with their tiny legs on their first bike with a firm hand on a shoulder)
reading your reviews of your favorite shows where you argue with the haters who just don't get what's so great about them
listening to your hundreds of covers that you sang along with the original vocalist instead of just the accompaniment because it feels less lonely that way
