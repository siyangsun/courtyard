---
title: Lack of slack leads to resource-constrained decision making
---
- picking up a curse early on in a slay the spire run just means you have to spend a removal on it
- playing strongest board in tft keeps doors open to using health as a resource in the future
- staying healthy, having energy, good habits etc and having some free time means you afford to make future greedy decisions with outsized benefits
- there are also probably tons of passive buffs from running at high current HP too (snowballing / tempo lifestyle)

very hard to see the marginal benefit of slack though
