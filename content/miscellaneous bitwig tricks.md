Tips and tricks for [[bitwig studio]]

- vocoder applies harmonic effect of a modulator onto the carrier signal
- playing around with note effects
	- microtonal pitch filters
	- dribble / ricochet
	- velocity curve
	- arpeggiator
	- bend
	- humanize
- modulators
	- ADSR modulator can sidechain reverb easily
	- knob to map multiple effects on one axis
	- XY to map multiple effects on two axes
- the grid
	- basic use of math to generate sequences
	- fx grid to get really crazy effects
	- just using the grid to set up multiple oscillators like any fm or wavetable synth

bitwig note expressions to map velocity/timbre to stuff
ADSR is also interesting for this, if u dont want to deal with automations being kind of messy to deal with (even with a macro or XY)

- alt to curve automations
