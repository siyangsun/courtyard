title: My hyperpop journey
---
2010 skrillex
2013/14 learn about odd future and start listening to hip hop
2010s listening to majority edm, slowly getting more experimental
2015/2016 randomly get into pre-88rising stuff (it g ma)
get introduced to LA beats scene
2016-2017 getting into mainstream rap at the time
2017 slightly more emo / underground with internet money, lyrical lemonade stuff
find suicideboys
start making beats a lot more around this time
peep, x, and juice world pass away

2018
sad emo rap time
simultaneously start listening to a lot of left field bass
listen to sophie and dont really get it yet

2019
start watching the cave, learn about jpegmafia and rico nasty
go more and more underground with hip hop
start watching patrick cc and listening to his scene (convolk guardin polearm shinigami glaive)
1000 gecs comes out, i think i see it from somebody on tinder's bio in jan 2020
but also i'm listening to dylan brady's stuff with josh pan without realizing it
i'm already indoctrinated into crazy sounding 808s and digital-ly sounds by this point

2020
listen to bladee when 333 comes out
learn about ag cook from 7G
listen to ecco2k and download whitearmor drum kits
give sophie album a deeper listen, get into pc music proper
discover underscores
discover iglooghost
