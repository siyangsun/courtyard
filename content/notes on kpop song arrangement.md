---
title: Notes on KPOP song arrangement
---

- TONS of different melodic elements come in but only as short fills / embellishments (temporally don't take up a lot of space) and blend into each other (e.g. a melody line is mostly piano but actually has a distorted guitar note at the end)
- 1st and 2nd time chorus show up it's the same, 3rd time it's with a few things dialed back but a new vocal line / background vocal
- lots of reversed vocal swells
- textures / instruments from other genres will show up for 4 or 8 bars (acid bassline, trance rave leads)
- white noise fills will have a textural element (rain, water splashes, car sounds, etc.) diegetic to the MV
- each set of 4 or 8 bars in the verse will have a different melodic instrument or style in the background for ear candy
- they love the drum loops with opening LP filter
- many different types of drum styles and patterns (in each set of 8 bars tbh)
- taking away elements at an unexpected time for transitions
