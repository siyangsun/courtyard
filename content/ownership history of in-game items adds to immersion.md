---
title: Ownership history of in-game items adds to immersion
---

In WoW, you can see who crafted your legendary items, as if they were made by a famous artisan or blacksmith; this adds a sense of immersion. What if you could also see all previous owners of a piece of gear? See the lineage as it was passed down?

What if in PoE you could see which players made every crafting change on a piece of gear? This would help prove legitimacy but also deepen the lore of that item.

What if you could stake your in-game items in a fight? It could function similarly to runescape duels, but the items themselves could be non-fungible. Something along the lines of Yugioh duels with rare hunters over rare cards.
