---
title: Printing as creative commitment
---


"Printing" is a lossy conversion of the flexible working state of a project, akin to (and sometimes literally referring to) actual physical printing. For example, flattening layers of a visual art file, or saving the audio/video of an expansive project.

- faster workflow, saves compute (mental or literal)
- forced to commit to decisions, creative limitation
- nimble to work in rendered media
- weird artifacts that you wouldnt think of
- can be taken to the max with infinite loops for unexpected behavior
