it's like processing abstract (body language, color of a sound, feelings and ideas tied to a place or experience) data in ur ETL process of gathering information about the world. and so you need to take time to process situations that others can kind of just take at face value. however these kind of abstract eigenvectors can help us approximate and understand nuanced situations practically instinctually (he yelled at me so he's mad vs -> the subtle facial expressions and word choice / how he reacted to himself yelling suggest he's more frustrated at himself, based on some similar-ish vibe i've experienced at the past and thought about)
