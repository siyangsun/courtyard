title: Pros and cons of moving out
---
I already decided but clearly need to hash this out so I can forget about it

Pros:
- Agency (feel like my own person, make my own decisions). think this is the biggest one by far
- Socialization
- Variety of sights and sounds and sensations
- Air is way less dry and more breathable
- Save some time from dumb conversations with fam (eg daily questions about work)
- Weather is perpetually nice, no snow or other bullshit
- Food variety
- Feel in a more "creative" environment
- Better non-work setup
- More consolidated when ordering stuff

Cons:
- Don't get to hang out with parents
- Spring is starting here
- Have to cook and clean
- Way less space indoors
- No private outdoor space that I can do whatever I want in
- Will get very hot in the summer
- Might feel pressured to hang out with people
