---
title: Roguelike games cause happy accidents
tags: []
---

Due to their generative nature, [roguelike games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roguelike) can cause [[happy accidents]] during gameplay. Since every run is different, certain combinations of circumstances can flip the rules on what decisions are "good". This encourages creative thinking based on solid understanding of the game's mechanics, rather than just memorizing one winning strategy.
