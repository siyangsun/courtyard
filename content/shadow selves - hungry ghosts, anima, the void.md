there are many sides to each of us. human beings are capable of multitudes, facets, dimensions. get a girl who can do both!

sometimes, though, there are sides that are so polarizing that it's a surprise they're a part of the same person. beyond just an alter ego, it can feel like there are multiple personalities inhabiting one body. these are often exaggerated to a literal sense in popular media, as illustrated by the late robert louis stevenson or the later alfred hitchcock or the latest kazuki takahashi.

- the shadow self represents desire, hunger, ambition, lust, destruction. an animalistic entity
	- hungry ghost in buddhism
	- the id in freud's philosophy
	- anima/animus in carl jung's philosophy
- modern society pushes us to suppress our shadow self, leading to maladaptive disorders
- the shadow self can be useful when briefly switched with
	- fuel/raw energy + confidence
	- different perspective. hungry ghost needs to root in selfish realism to not starve
		- deleting excuses
		- setting boundaries
	- executive function. do it bc it must be done
