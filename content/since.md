The time since I've been doing ...

## Obsidian
I started using [[obsidian]] as a second brain and journal in June 2020. I started publishing a portion of these notes via quartz in January or February of 2022.

## Journaling / Blogging
I probably kept a diary at some point as a kid, but I don't remember much about it. I was honestly too busy leaving traces of myself online instead.
In 11th grade English class, the honors students were assigned The Art of the Personal Essay as required reading. One of the teachers (not mine) had all of his students keep a Wordpress blog that semester as an accompanying exercise on essay writing, and I was inspired to do the same. This blog lasted for a few months. I created another one the following year and kept it decently updated until 2016. 

## Retroactive calendar
I keep record of everything I do by adding calendar events after the fact (if an event wasn't created already). It appears I started doing this in January 2019.

## Programming
I first "learned" javascript some time in elementary at the suggestion of my mother. This was repeated in 6th grade computer class, where we learned how to make a simple radio button dialog box. In early high school I attended a Python programming camp run by my local math circle. Throughout college I took courses on programming fundamentals in C++, data structures and algorithms, Python and R specific courses, and machine learning, but I honestly didn't feel fully fluent until starting various research projects and internships around 2017-2019.

## Music production
I started composing music in 6th grade when my friend and I begged our parents to buy us Finale software. In 2010 or 2011 I found a cracked version of FL Studio 10 and started trying to make dubstep on it as I had just discovered Skrillex at the time. That stuff was really horrible and I had to set it to private on Soundcloud. In 2016 I was encouraged to work towards a hobby goal within a group of peers, and I chose music production; for the next few years I would make lofi hip hop and trap beats (and only post the lofi hip hop). During the pandemic I dove much deeper into making music, and also switched over to [[bitwig studio]].

## Drawing
I started drawing dinosaurs about as early as I can remember. In 1st grade my parents sent me to a drawing class where we used pastels. In middle school they bought me my first Wacom tablet for digital art, where I used MS Paint and Adobe Photoshop Elements. I was very active on Elfwood and DeviantArt around that time (mid-late 2000s). I would continue creating digital art and sharing on regular social media until 2017, when I created dedicated art accounts on Instagram and Tumblr. I also started a university club for drawing with some of my friends around this time that lasted for about 2 years. In 2021 I switched over to an XP Pen Display Tablet. In 2022 I enrolled in an online program called the [[abstract design theory course]]. Throughout 2024 I started going to in-person figure drawing classes and trying to draw / paint more traditionally as well.

## Swimming
I learned how to swim as a young child at a public pool in Toronto. When I was 9 I signed up for my local town summer swim league (Rock Creek Flyers) and performed very poorly. I would do this every summer until I was 12. Years later I swam for my high school's varsity team for the entirety of my time there, and eventually broke a 30 second 50m freestyle (see, I never said I was fast). I tried out for club in college but found the uphill commute back from the pool too bothersome. I didn't swim regularly for a while after that. In 2023 my roommate signed up for the Alcatraz Escape with minimal swimming experience, and I felt some compulsion to help him train a bit. This quickly reignited my own relationship with swimming. I found a YMCA close by with a lap pool and began trying to go a couple times a week. I was thankfully (and also frustratingly) just as fast as before despite taking many years off.
