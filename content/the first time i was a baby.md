---
title: The first time I was a baby
---
the first time i was a baby,
my parents were literal gods
they created the world around me, procuring food and wine, dictating the rules of the earth
as i grew up, they became more and more human to me

the second time (having projected myself through my imagination and dreams) i realized a parallel truth
they were just some 28 year olds who i forced to grow up and taught how to love unconditionally
i was the god
my existence re-created the world around them, mandated them to procure food and wine, and required them to dictate the rules
i changed their relationship
i changed their perspective on their relationship
and on responsibility
and on giving
and on ego
and on life (as the hundred year journey one goes through. as the beautiful energy that powers living things)
and as i grew up, i became more and more human to them
