- $150 seems like a lot but i also just blew like $90 on dinner so like...
- some of the initial workflow improvements would probably be captured just by using a lot more presets on my other synths and loosely dragging in one or two note hits per synth instead of trying to overload phrases with one layer
- or trying random preset effects
- doing all this and **resampling / editing audio**. THIS IS PROB A BIG ONE
	- very cool putting loops in it rather than kicks. just getting a weird mud pie and slicing that up as a *sample*
	- resample into a oneshot that can be played as a note!
- i think this results in being able to actually slow down the pace a lil bit when doing exploration (without losing momentum). leave it more open to serendipity and working as hands on as possible; rather than trying too hard to architect elements from the very beginning when there isnt even anything to work with
- serendipity first when doing sound design!! or maybe serendipity first always..
#### i guess the main things about synplant are that:
- sometimes generates some reallyyyy weird shit (which is low key unusable unless it's just in a oneshot or resampled into something)
- you literally never run out of presets
- you can generate more presets "similar" to a sample or existing preset
- can reverse engineer FM synth out of sounds i guess?
