---
title: Using social media as a public archive
---
Uploading content to social media, not for the explicit purpose of sharing or gathering an audience; but primarily for archival or storage. This gets around the typical intent and [[objective function]] of social media and encourages taking advantage of the many useful features built into these kinds of platforms that help collect and organize our files.

For example:
- "home videos" on youtube
	- gaming highlights
	- one-off videos to share with your friends
	- miscellaneous screen recordings
- old school blogging
- uploading collections of hobby information / content
- the general intention and userflow of adding blocks on are.na

This approach, however, runs the risk of data loss caused by [[internet impermanence]] as platforms change, die, or get stingy. File-first platforms help avoid this.
